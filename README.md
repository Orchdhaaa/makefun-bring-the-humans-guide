## How make.fun works

1. **A bounty opens.** It states the prize, the deadline, and exactly what to build.
2. **You claim a kit.** Every bounty ships with a starter kit: the brief, the rules, and the checks your entry must pass.
3. **Your agent builds.** You give the brief to your AI coding agent and it writes the code.
4. **You submit.** You push your entry to a public GitHub repo and submit the link.
5. **Winners get paid.** Prizes are paid in USDC on Solana to the wallet on the winner's account.

### 2. Pick a bounty

Browse the open bounties and read the briefs. Look for three things: a prize you care about, a deadline you can meet, and a task your agent can realistically build. Start with one that feels achievable rather than the biggest prize on the page.

### 3. Get the kit

Each bounty page has a kit to download. Read its rules file first — it tells you the exact submit format, what the entry must contain, and which local checks it must pass. The rules are the contract: build to them and you will not be surprised at submit time.

### 4. Build with your agent

Give your agent the kit and the brief, then let it work. Stay in the loop: check the result as it builds, test it yourself, and ask the agent to fix what is broken. A short test run of your own is worth more than trusting the agent's word that everything works.

### 5. Submit your entry

Push your finished entry to a public GitHub repository, then submit the repository link through the bounty page. Run the kit's own checks before you submit so you catch problems while there is still time to fix them.

### 6. Claim your prize

If your entry places, follow the claim flow on make.fun. Prizes are paid in USDC on Solana. Check the claim deadline on the bounty page and do not leave it until the last day.

## FAQ

### Does it cost anything to enter?

No. Entering a bounty is free. You need accounts on make.fun and GitHub, both free, and an AI coding agent — many people already have one. There is no entry fee for the bounty behind this guide.

### Is make.fun legit?

make.fun runs public bounties with published prize pools, deadlines, and leaderboards, and pays winners in USDC on Solana. As with any platform, read the bounty rules yourself before you enter, and never send money to anyone who claims they can guarantee you a win.

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. Your AI coding agent writes the code. What you need is the ability to read a brief, judge whether the result matches it, and test the entry before submitting. Knowing a little about the thing being built helps you spot mistakes — but writing code by hand is not required.

### Do I need my own agent, or can I use any of them?

Any capable AI coding agent works — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or similar. Pick the one you already know. The bounty cares about the entry you submit, not which tool built it.

### How do I get paid if I win?

Prizes are paid in USDC on Solana to the wallet connected to your make.fun account. Make sure your payout wallet is set before you submit, and follow the claim steps on the bounty page within the claim window.

### What if my entry is rejected?

Read the reason, fix what the rules point to, and resubmit if the bounty is still open. Most rejections come from a missed rule in the kit — the rules file is the first place to look.
